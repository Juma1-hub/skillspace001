Availability, eligibility and features may change.

      2. Accounts and accurate information

      You must provide accurate information, keep your account details up to date, and protect your login credentials. You are responsible for activity performed through your account. Tell SKILLSPACE promptly if you suspect unauthorized access. Do not impersonate another person, create accounts to evade limits, or misrepresent your skills, identity or payment status.

      3. Memberships and access

      Membership access is determined by the plan associated with your account. Public visitors may see categories, while account holders can access member features according to their plan. Free, Basic, Standard and Premium have different task allowances and access levels. Premium is required for Academy learning materials. A user may see a listing that their current plan does not allow them to open or submit; an upgrade may be required. Plan prices are shown in USD and may also show an approximate KSh equivalent. The KSh figure is indicative and can change with exchange rates or payment-provider charges.

      4. Tasks, submissions and review

      Read the instructions, scope, deadline, budget and required deliverables before starting a task. Submit original work that follows the task brief and respects copyright, confidentiality, privacy and applicable law. Submissions are pending review until an authorized administrator approves or rejects them. A submitted item is not approved merely because it was uploaded or recorded. Review outcomes should be based on the task requirements; where appropriate, feedback may be provided. Do not submit harmful files, plagiarized work, fabricated results, or personal information collected without permission.

      5. Services and client requests

      Service descriptions identify typical deliverables and indicative starting prices. Confirm the final scope, delivery time, revisions and price before committing to a service. Do not use the platform to request or provide illegal, deceptive, abusive, discriminatory or infringing services. SKILLSPACE may remove listings or restrict accounts to protect members and platform integrity.

      6. Payments, refunds and withdrawals

      Paid membership is not active until the payment is verified by the authorized payment system and the account is updated accordingly. Entering a phone number or viewing a payment-pending message is not proof of payment. M-Pesa processing, where enabled, will be subject to Safaricom and payment-provider terms. Never share your M-Pesa PIN or one-time passcodes with SKILLSPACE or another user. Fees, refunds, disputes, withdrawal minimums and timelines must be disclosed before a live transaction is enabled. The current browser-only prototype does not itself initiate or verify real M-Pesa transactions. Referral programme: a member may earn a commission equal to 25% of the membership amount actually paid by a client attributed to their referral code, after the payment has been verified. No commission is earned for a free plan, an unverified or failed payment, a self-referral, or a payment that is refunded or reversed. The browser-only prototype records demonstration commissions locally and does not itself transfer or pay out money.

      7. Acceptable use and intellectual property

      Do not attempt to bypass plan limits, interfere with site security, scrape private data, distribute malware, manipulate reviews or submissions, harass other users, or misuse referrals. You retain rights to materials you create, subject to any separate agreement with a client. You must have permission to upload or share any third-party content. You grant SKILLSPACE only the limited permission needed to host and display content for operating the service, where that functionality is enabled.

      8. Suspension and termination

      SKILLSPACE may restrict or suspend access when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, security issues, unlawful activity or serious violations of these terms. Where practicable, the platform will communicate the reason and provide a way to raise a concern. You may stop using the service and request account or personal-data action through the support channel, subject to lawful retention obligations.

      9. Availability and disclaimers

      The platform is provided subject to availability. We will take reasonable steps to maintain accurate listings and protect the service, but cannot promise uninterrupted access, specific earnings, task availability, client outcomes or error-free operation. Browser-only demonstrations may not synchronize across devices and should not be used to store sensitive information.

      Information may be used to create and manage accounts, show plan-appropriate features, administer task allowances, receive and review submissions, respond to service and support requests, manage membership payments, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain platform security, resolve disputes, and improve the service. Anyone who can access the same browser profile or device may be able to inspect local records. Do not use real passwords or upload confidential or sensitive information in this demo.
